The conversion factors

All factors are exact definitions, so the only rounding is in the display: 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N, 1 kgf = 9.80665 N, 1 kip = 1000 lbf, 1 in = 25.4 mm. Tables are rounded to the nearest whole lbf or kgf; the converter shows up to four decimals.

How the conversion works

Load = gauge pressure × ram effective area. With pressure in pascals and area in square metres the result is in newtons; the tool converts units with exact definitions (1 psi = 1 lbf/in², 1 bar = 100 kPa, 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N, 1 in = 25.4 mm). Two quick checks: 1,000 psi on a 1 in² ram is 1,000 lbf, and 100 bar on a 10 cm² ram is 10 kN.

This is the ideal relation. Seal friction and gauge error make a real tester read differently, which is why testers are calibrated. Where the calibration certificate gives a pressure-to-load table, use that table, and use this tool for planning and cross-checks. The tool does not choose loads or limits; those come from your spec.

Effective area from measured diameters: solid A = π × D² / 4, hollow A = π × (D² − d²) / 4. The tester manufacturer's stated effective area or calibration table takes precedence. Calculated with the free tool at calyx-ai.net/qa/hydraulic-ram-effective-area/ (Quality Tester Solutions LLC).

How it works

Solid piston: A = π × D² / 4. Hollow ram: A = π × (D² − d²) / 4, the ring between the piston diameter and the inner seal diameter. Ideal load = gauge pressure × A. Unit factors are exact (1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 psi = 1 lbf/in², 1 bar = 100 kPa, 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N). Two checks: a 1.128 in solid piston has about 1.0 in², so 1,000 psi gives about 1,000 lbf; a hollow ram with D = 50 mm and d = 20 mm has 1,649.34 mm², so 100 bar gives 16.49 kN.

Measured diameters give an estimate. Seal friction and gauge error make a real tester read differently, which is why testers are calibrated. Where the manufacturer states the effective area, or the calibration certificate gives a pressure-to-load table, use those, and use this page for cross-checks.

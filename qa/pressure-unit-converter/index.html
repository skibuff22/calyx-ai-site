The conversion factors

All factors come from exact definitions, so the only rounding is in the display: 1 psi = 1 lbf/in² = 6,894.757 Pa (1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N, 1 in = 25.4 mm), 1 bar = 100 kPa = 0.1 MPa, 1 kgf/cm² = 98.0665 kPa. The psi table is rounded to 0.01 bar and 0.001 MPa, the bar table to whole psi; the converter shows up to four decimals.

This page converts gauge units only. To turn a gauge reading into load you also need the ram's effective area: the gauge pressure to load calculator does the conversion and prints a load chart, and the ram effective area calculator estimates the area from the piston and inner seal diameters. For stress results (MPa, psi, ksi) use the stress unit converter.

How it works

The clock counts from the moment you press Start, using the device clock, so a slow screen refresh never makes it drift. A row is added at 0:00 and at every reading time (30 s, 60 s, … with a 30-second interval) up to the end of the hold, which also gets a row; "Log reading now" adds a row at the current time, for example when the load drops and you re-pump. Pause stops the clock and the reading prompts; Reset clears the clock and the rows. After the hold time is reached the clock keeps counting the overrun.

Keep this tab in front while timing: phones and browsers slow down or silence background tabs, so a beep can come late even though the times in the log stay correct. Where the browser supports it, the page asks to keep the screen on while the timer runs. The hold time, reading times and the displacement limit come from your spec or the anchor manufacturer; the tool does not choose them. To check the readings against a limit, enter them in the displacement under load check; for mm and inches, the displacement unit converter; for the full record of the test, the pull-test record sheet.

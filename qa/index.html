Who it is for

Field technicians, inspectors and QA staff who run proof or ultimate pull tests on post-installed anchors, fasteners in wood or masonry, and similar jobs, and who now write the report by hand or rebuild a spreadsheet for every job. The templates work with any pull tester.

The seller, Quality Tester Solutions LLC, is the company behind Force-Test, which makes fastener pull testers.

What the templates do not do

They record and summarise your results. Acceptance criteria, load levels, sample sizes and the k factor for characteristic values come from your specification, the anchor manufacturer and the governing standard; enter them in the yellow cells. Check the current edition of any standard with its publisher.

How it is calculated

Anchors to test = installed × share ÷ 100, rounded up to a whole anchor. If you enter a minimum and the share gives fewer, the minimum is used. The count never exceeds the number installed. Proof load = factor × working load, shown in kN and lbf (1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N).

Where the numbers come from

This calculator does not choose the share, the minimum or the factor. They depend on the anchor type, the base material, whether the anchors are safety-critical and the document that governs the job, so take them from the specification, the anchor manufacturer or the engineer of record. Which anchors to pick and what counts as a pass come from the same place. Check the current edition of any standard with its publisher.

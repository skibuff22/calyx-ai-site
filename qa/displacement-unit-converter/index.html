The conversion factors

The inch is defined as exactly 25.4 mm, so every factor here is exact and the only rounding is in the display: 1 mm = 0.03937008 in = 39.37008 mil, 1 mil (thou) = 0.001 in = 0.0254 mm = 25.4 µm, 1 µm = 0.001 mm, 1 cm = 10 mm. The mm table is rounded to 0.0001 in and 0.1 mil, the inch table to 0.001 mm; the converter shows up to six decimals.

This page converts units only; the displacement limit comes from your spec or the anchor manufacturer. To check readings taken during a hold against a limit, use the displacement under load check; to time the hold, the proof-load hold timer; for loads, the force unit converter.

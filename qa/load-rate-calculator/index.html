How it works

Time = (target load − start load) / rate, assuming the load rises at a constant rate. A rate per second is multiplied by 60 for the per-minute value. Steps divide the load evenly from the start load to the target, and each step's time is its share of the total. Unit factor is exact: 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N.

The tool does not choose the test load, the rate, the seating load or the hold time; those come from your spec or the standard it names.

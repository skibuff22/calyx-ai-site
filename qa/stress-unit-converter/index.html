The conversion factors

All factors come from exact definitions, so the only rounding is in the display: 1 MPa = 1 N/mm² = 145.0377 psi, 1 psi = 0.006894757 MPa (1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N, 1 in = 25.4 mm), 1 ksi = 1,000 psi, 1 kgf/cm² = 0.0980665 MPa. The MPa table is rounded to whole psi and 0.01 ksi, the psi table to 0.001 MPa; the converter shows up to four decimals.

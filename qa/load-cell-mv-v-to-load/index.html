How it works

A strain-gauge load cell's rated output (mV/V) is its signal at rated capacity for each volt of excitation, so the full-scale signal is rated output × excitation. The tool assumes a straight line through the zero offset: load = (signal − zero offset) / (rated output × excitation) × capacity, and expected signal = zero offset + load / capacity × rated output × excitation. Check: a 50 kN cell with 2 mV/V at 10 V excitation gives 20 mV full scale; 8 mV with zero offset 0 is 20 kN (40 % of capacity), and a 30 kN target gives 12 mV.

A straight line is an approximation. When the calibration certificate gives its own coefficients or a load table, use those; the tool does not correct for non-linearity, hysteresis, temperature or cable effects. A reading above 100 % of capacity is flagged.

How it is calculated

Mean is the arithmetic average. s is the sample standard deviation (divides by n − 1, the same as STDEV / STDEV.S in Excel), so it needs at least two results. Coefficient of variation is s divided by the mean. The characteristic value is mean − k × s using the k you enter; the minimum / design ratio is the lowest result divided by the design load you enter.

Where k comes from

This calculator does not choose k for you. The factor depends on the number of tests, the confidence level and the fractile your document requires, so take it from the standard or specification governing the job, or from the engineer of record. The same goes for how many anchors to test and what result is acceptable. Check the current edition with its publisher.
